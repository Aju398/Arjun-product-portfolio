Case Study 1

Project: SureScan | Insurance Intelligence MVP

Role: Lead Product Manager

▶ [Explain Video: SureScan Walkthrough]

The Challenge

Insurance policies in India are 100-page "Black Boxes." Users often discover hidden exclusions only during a medical emergency, leading to a massive trust deficit in the industry.

Key Impact

  • Time-to-Clarity: 45 min → 3 min.
  • Accountability: AI summary linked to original PDF clause.
Feature Logic & Business Rules
Jargon-Buster IF term = [Legal Jargon], THEN display [Plain English] (e.g. "Capping" → "Room Rent Limit").
Exclusion Finder AI scans for "Waiting Periods" and "Sub-limits"; triggers High-Alert Red UI.
Claim-Ready Score Proprietary 1-10 score based on Claim Ratio, Hospital Proximity, and Transparency.
Case Study 2

Zerodha Coin | SIP Transparency & Analytics

Focus: Product Growth & Retention

▶ [Explain Video: Proposed SIP Dashboard]

The Problem

Zerodha Coin simplifies investing but lacks performance visibility. Users see a "Total Portfolio" view but cannot see how individual SIP installments are performing or exactly when units are allotted.

Expected Success

  • 15% Increase in SIP retention.
  • 30% Reduction in "NAV anxiety" support tickets.

SIP Cohort Dashboard

Treats each monthly installment as a separate entry to visualize the power of Rupee Cost Averaging during market volatility.

Execution Timeline

Progress Bar: Bank Debit → Order Sent → NAV Applied → Unit Allotment. Total transparency on investment status.
