Case Study 01

SureScan: Solving the Insurance "Black Box"

Product: Insurance Intelligence MVP • Role: Lead PM

⚡ Video Walkthrough Placeholder

The Problem

Indian insurance policies are 100-page legal documents. Users face "Claim Surprises" because exclusions are buried in fine print. SureScan converts these into a 1-page "Pulse Report."

Key Success Metrics

  • Time-to-Clarity: 45m → 3m
  • Accuracy: 100% Citation linkage
Feature Module Logic & Functional Rules
Jargon-Buster IF term match [Legal_Library], THEN tooltip [Plain_English].
Exclusion Radar AI scans for sub-limits; highlights waiting periods in Alert Red.
Claim-Ready Score Proprietary 1-10 ranking based on Settlement Ratios & Transparency.
Case Study 02

Zerodha Coin: SIP Performance & Transparency

Domain: WealthTech • Goal: Improvement & Retention

📈 Product Improvement Demo Placeholder

The Friction

Coin lacks granular performance tracking for individual SIP cohorts. Users experience "NAV Anxiety" due to a lack of visibility during the bank-to-AMC deployment cycle.

Expected Impact

  • +15% SIP Retention rate
  • -30% Support tickets on NAV status

SIP Cohort Dashboard

Individualized installment tracking to visualize the benefit of Rupee Cost Averaging over time.

Execution Timeline

A "Track My Order" style flow: Bank Debit → Order Sent → NAV Applied → Unit Allotment.
