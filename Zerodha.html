Zerodha Coin – SIP Transparency Improvement

Zerodha Coin allows investors to run SIPs into mutual funds, but users often lack clarity about execution timing and NAV allocation. This case study proposes improvements to the SIP tracking experience through cohort performance analysis and deployment transparency.

The redesign introduces a SIP cohort dashboard, showing investors exactly how each SIP installment performs over time and at which NAV it was deployed.

Product Demo

Product Thinking

Problem

Investors using SIPs can access NAV, transaction history, and portfolio returns, but these exist as disconnected data points. There is no clear linkage between when an investment was made, the market conditions at that time, and how it contributed to overall portfolio growth. As a result, investors can see what happened, but not why it happened, leading to passive behavior, low conviction, and emotionally driven decisions during market fluctuations.

Key Insight

The gap is not in data availability but in causality. Investors do not lack information—they lack context. They think of SIPs as a continuous investment journey, but current platforms present them as isolated transactions. What users truly want is to understand how each SIP installment impacts their portfolio and whether their investment timing is helping or hurting their returns.

Solution

Introduce a SIP Intelligence Layer that connects investment timing, NAV allocation, market conditions, and portfolio growth. This includes a causality graph that maps each SIP installment to its contribution, a cohort analytics view to compare performance over time, a heatmap to visualize investment efficiency, and AI-driven insights that guide users on whether to continue, adjust, or review their SIP strategy. The feature is integrated into the portfolio via a “View SIP Insights” entry point.

Impact

This enhancement transforms SIP investing from passive tracking into insight-driven decision-making. By helping users understand the cause-and-effect relationship in their investments, it builds trust, increases confidence, and encourages better financial behavior. Expected outcomes include higher SIP continuation rates, improved engagement with analytics, and more informed investment decisions across market cycles.
