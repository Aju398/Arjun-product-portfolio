Zerodha Coin – SIP Transparency Improvement

Zerodha Coin allows investors to run SIPs into mutual funds, but users often lack clarity about execution timing and NAV allocation. This case study proposes improvements to the SIP tracking experience through cohort performance analysis and deployment transparency.

The redesign introduces a SIP cohort dashboard, showing investors exactly how each SIP installment performs over time and at which NAV it was deployed.

Product Demo

Product Thinking

Problem

Investors running SIPs often lack transparency about when their investments are executed and which NAV they receive.

Insight

Users think about SIPs as a timeline of investments, but the current UI treats them as individual transactions without context.

Solution

Introduce a SIP cohort dashboard that groups installments together and visualizes performance across time.

Impact

Improved transparency builds investor trust and can increase long-term SIP retention.
