SureScan – Insurance Policy Analyzer

SureScan simplifies the process of understanding insurance policies by converting complex PDF documents into a clear and concise risk summary. Users upload their insurance policy and the system extracts exclusions, risk clauses, and claim conditions using AI powered document intelligence.

The goal of the product is to reduce the time required to understand a policy from nearly an hour to under three minutes while helping users identify hidden risks.

Product Demo

Product Thinking

Problem

Insurance policies are long, complex documents that most users struggle to understand, especially when identifying exclusions and claim conditions.

Insight

Users typically read policies only when they need to file a claim, which means critical risk clauses are often overlooked.

Solution

Build an AI-powered analyzer that extracts exclusions, risks, and claim triggers from the policy and summarizes them in a simple one-page report.

Impact

Reducing policy analysis time from nearly an hour to a few minutes improves user understanding and lowers the likelihood of claim surprises.
