SureScan – Insurance Policy Analyzer

SureScan simplifies the process of understanding insurance policies by converting complex PDF documents into a clear and concise risk summary. Users upload their insurance policy and the system extracts exclusions, risk clauses, and claim conditions using AI powered document intelligence.

The goal of the product is to reduce the time required to understand a policy from nearly an hour to under three minutes while helping users identify hidden risks.
